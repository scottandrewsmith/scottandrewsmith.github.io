Bio

I'm an associate professor at the Academy of Mathematics and Systems Sciences within the Chinese Academy of Sciences in Beijing. My interest in mathematics started at the University of Maryland, where I received my Bachelor's in 2011 and PhD in 2016. Most of my recent work concerns singular stochastic PDEs and related problems in mathematical physics.
